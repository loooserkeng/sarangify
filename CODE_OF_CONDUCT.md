# Code of Conduct

SARANGIFY welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and experience. Everyone taking part in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful, constructive, and inclusive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Offer constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Accept differing viewpoints and respond to disagreement professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the repository maintainers through [GitHub](https://github.com/saranghaeyong/sarangify) to report conduct concerns. Please do not post private or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as possible.

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions or participation that violate this code.
